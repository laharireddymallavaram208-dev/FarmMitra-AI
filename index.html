Rice Blast (వరి అగ్గితెగులు)

Pathogen: Magnaporthe oryzae (Fungus)
94%
Match Confidence
Risk Level: High Risk

Identified Probable Causes:

Excess nitrogenous fertilizer, continuous high humidity above 90%, and cool night temperatures favour fungal spore germination.

Immediate Field Action:

Immediately drain excess stagnant water from the field. Temporarily pause top-dressing of urea/nitrogen fertilizers.

Organic & Biological Remedies:

Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or 5ml Neem Oil per liter with soap surfactant. Soil application of Pseudomonas fluorescens (2.5 kg/ha).

Recommended Safe Chemical Control:

Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6g/L or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5ml/L of clean water during early morning hours.

Prevention for Next Crop Season:

Use certified blast-resistant seed varieties (such as MTU-1010, BPT-5204 treated) and perform seed treatment with Carbendazim (2g/kg seed).

Why We Built This

Empowering Indian Farmers with Accessible AI

Over 82% of Indian farmers are smallholders operating on less than 2 hectares of land. When sudden pests strike or irregular weather hits, they often travel miles to input dealers who sell expensive chemicals.

FarmRakshak AI (రైతు రక్షక్) was conceptualized during this college hackathon as a lightweight, zero-barrier, digital agronomy assistant. It works on low-cost smartphones without costly hardware, offering guidance in regional languages like Telugu alongside English.

Zero Cost, Zero Dependencies:

Runs purely in modern web browsers without requiring credit cards or paid cloud API keys.

Bilingual Inclusivity:

Instant one-click toggle between English and Telugu ensures local farmers can understand every advisory.

Climate-Resilient Agriculture:

Promotes natural formulations like Jeevamrutha, Neem sprays, and efficient drip scheduling.
